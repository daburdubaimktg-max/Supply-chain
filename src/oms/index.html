How the platform runs

Each team owns one sheet; the dashboard reads all of them

1 · Logistics
Order Tracker
Log each distributor order: country, brand, FG code, quantity, date. Update reserved and supplied when stock moves.
2 · Planning
Order Summary
Allocation runs by country priority, only from each country's allowed locations and within its age norm, oldest lot first. Pending quantities go to Production; the planned quantities come back and are shared with all stakeholders.
3 · Production
Plan or flag shortage
If RM and PM are available, enter production planned and produced. Otherwise log the RMPM shortage.
4 · RM / PM planners
Connectivity ETA
Saurav Nagrale (RM) and Maslekar Purushottam Parth (PM) enter the procurement ETA against each shortage. Arun Muthian (FG) keeps batch and storage location stock current so allocation runs on real lots.
5 · Production
Plan to connectivity
Once materials are connected, the planned quantity is entered against the FG and the order summary updates automatically.

Who can update the files

Editors
Logistics, Planning, Production, FG planner Arun Muthian (ageing and SLOB files), RM and PM planners
Share the tracker with these people as Can edit. They can upload files on the Supply Chain Inputs tab, add rows, change cells and download sheets.
Viewers
Country managers, brand managers, other stakeholders
Share as Can view. They see the live dashboard and every sheet and can download, but cannot change anything. The shared store refuses their writes.
Admin (page owner)
Sharing is managed from the share menu on this page
Only the admin can change the owner name shown against each upload on the Supply Chain Inputs tab. Access is checked when the page opens; a viewer sees "View only" in the left rail.

Core inputs

All uploaded on the Supply Chain Inputs tab.

  • FG ageing file from the FG Planner, Arun Muthian [daily]. Columns: FG Code, FG Description, Stoc Loc, Stoc Loc Description, Manuf Date, Expiry Date, Ageing Remarks. Add Batch and Available Qty per line so each lot can be allocated; without a quantity column the lots load with zero available.
  • FG SLOB file (slow-moving and obsolete stock, in cases) from the FG Planner, Arun Muthian [weekly]. Columns: FG Code, FG Description, Stoc Loc, Batch, Manuf Date, Expiry Date, SLOB Qty (Cases), Case Rate (USD) (optional; the order tracker ACR is used when blank), SLOB Category, Remarks. Shown in the Liquidation view with SLOB value.
  • Country priority and norms [monthly or when changed]. Columns: Country, Priority (1, 2 or 3), Allocate From (Stoc Loc codes or descriptions, comma separated; blank = any), Max Age Days (oldest inventory the country accepts), Min Shelf Life Days (least remaining life at dispatch). Blank = any.
  • FG shortage list: what FG will be short and by how much [weekly]. Columns: FG Code, Short Qty, Reason.
  • Production plan: production planned and produced per FG code [weekly, and on every change]. Columns: FG Code, Description, Production Planned, Produced.
  • RM and PM shortage list by material [weekly]. Columns: Material Code, Material Description, Type (RM or PM), Shortage Qty, UOM, ETA.
  • Procurement ETA per short material, entered by the RM or PM planner [as received].
  • Supply confirmation: supplied quantity, supply date and invoice or dispatch reference against each order [as dispatched]. This is what sets the supplied date on the dashboard.

Master data and plan inputs

Also uploaded on the Supply Chain Inputs tab. Each one switches on a feature.

  • FG master: FG code, description, brand, category, cases per pallet, MOQ, shelf life. Fills brand and description on any order, plan or SLOB line that only has the code.
  • Bill of materials mapping FG to RM and PM codes. Links the material-level RMPM sheet back to the FG codes and orders it blocks, with the material ETA.
  • In-transit plan: dispatch date, mode and ETA at destination per order, shown on the order timeline so country managers see arrival, not just dispatch.
  • Expiry date is already part of the FG ageing file, so the age norm can also be expressed as minimum remaining shelf life.
  • Sales forecast by country, brand, FG and month, in cases. Shown against ordered on the dashboard so demand not yet ordered is visible.

Refresh routine

  • Logistics updates the tracker as orders and dispatches happen.
  • Supply uploads stock every morning; planning re-checks the summary after the upload.
  • Production and RMPM sheets refresh weekly at the planning meeting and on every ETA change.
